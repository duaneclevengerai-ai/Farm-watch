Questions

Straight answers.

Do I need sensors or any hardware?

No. Farm Watch reads free Sentinel-2 satellite imagery for your field boundary and combines it with local weather.

Do I still control my farm?

Yes. You get your own login to see every field and change settings, log irrigation and decide what happens. We monitor and advise. You decide.

Can I connect my existing soil probes?

Yes. Upload a CSV from your probe system, or send readings automatically from any logger or gateway that can post data over the internet. We show your probes next to the satellite and check whether the two agree. We can help you set it up.

Which satellites do you use?

Sentinel-2 optical imagery for moisture and crop greenness, plus Sentinel-1 radar, which sees through cloud, currently in beta. Both come from the free European Copernicus programme.

How often does it update?

Weather and advice refresh daily. A new satellite reading arrives with each clear pass, about every five days. Clouds can delay a reading, and the app shows how old the latest one is.

How accurate is the advice?

It is an estimate from satellite data, weather, your crop, soil and irrigation method. It improves when you tell us whether it matched your field. It is guidance, so use your own judgement and local knowledge.

Which crops are supported?

More than 60, including almonds, pistachios, corn, cotton and wheat. If yours is missing, we use sensible defaults.

What does the alumni price mean?

The first 25 farms lock in the alumni rate. It will not go up for as long as you stay with us.

Who can see my data?

You, and the A1 AI team who manage your account. We do not sell data or show ads. You can delete your account at any time. See the Privacy Policy.
